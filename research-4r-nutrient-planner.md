# 4R Nutrient Planner — Research Basis

The FormulaAtlas 4R Nutrient Budget and Application Planner is a **planning aid**, not a regulatory fertilizer prescription. Its source, rate, time, and place framing is based on USDA NRCS nutrient-management guidance.

> “Nutrient Management is a sustainable approach to delivering the right nutrients, at the right rate, at the right time, and to the right place, to maximize economic benefits while minimizing environmental impact.” — USDA NRCS [1]

The interface therefore makes crop assumptions, soil and organic-source credits, and waterway-setback checks explicit. It retains user control over inputs and warns that local soil/source analyses, label directions, weather, conservation requirements, and regulations must be checked before field application.

## Product implications implemented

| 4R factor | Planner implementation |
|---|---|
| Right source | Stage-specific source starting points, optional manure source, and transparent first-year nutrient credit. |
| Right rate | Crop lifecycle target adjusted by a yield percentage, with soil organic matter, potassium, and manure credits displayed separately. |
| Right time | A dated crop-stage application schedule adjusted after planning credits, with low-CEC split-application guidance. |
| Right place | Stage-specific placement method, slope-sensitive planning buffer, and waterway-distance check. |
